## Selection criteria

This research does not rely on SEO rankings, random prompt collections, or social
posts. Codex behavior and skills are moving targets, so vendor materials are more
reliable here than secondary summaries. Future research should add domain-specific
primary sources when its subject is a particular industry, standard, or technology.
