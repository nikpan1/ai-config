Payments notes for the fictional repair appointment service.

The service records payment status and the payment reference. It does not store full card numbers. A failed payment does not by itself cancel a confirmed appointment. If the payment-status service times out, support may request one status refresh; this permission does not authorize retrying a card charge.

An approved refund is sent to the original payment method. Approval and settlement are separate events. The service does not promise when the receiving bank will make funds available.

Two current desk instructions conflict about the service's own submission deadline for an approved refund. Instruction A says: submit within two working days after approval. Instruction B says: submit within five working days after approval. Neither instruction supplies a precedence rule. Preserve both deadlines and the unresolved difference; neither deadline describes bank settlement.

For an unpaid booking, a fee waiver is a cancellation of the fee obligation, not a payment or a refund. A customer's compensation request requires a separate decision. No amount or automatic entitlement is specified here.
