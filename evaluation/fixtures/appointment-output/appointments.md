The repair appointment service accepts bookings for home visits. This is fictional test material.

A confirmed address and a contact number are required before a booking can be confirmed. Providing both does not guarantee an appointment: the customer must receive a separate confirmation. A request records the customer's preferred slot; it does not reserve that slot.

Changes and cancellations:
- A customer can request a change until 18:00 local office time on the day before the confirmed visit. The old slot remains confirmed until a replacement is confirmed. An unsuccessful change request does not cancel the old visit.
- A cancellation received at least 24 hours before the confirmed start is eligible for review for a full refund. Eligibility for review is not approval of a refund.
- Inside that 24-hour window, the standard cancellation fee is 20 credits. A supervisor may waive this fee when the customer provides evidence of a service outage. The waiver removes the fee; it does not approve any separate compensation request.

If the technician cancels, the customer can choose a new slot or a full refund. A reschedule is not automatic. These technician-cancellation options do not establish the options for a customer who cancels.

Refund processing is described in [Payments](payments.md). The premium-visit proposal is not approved and must not be offered as an available service. A booking with a safety concern requires review before a technician is dispatched; a concern is not a finding that the home is unsafe.
