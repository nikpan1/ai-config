# Identity at collection

This is synthetic acceptance-test documentation.

The collection identity check compares the reservation name with an unexpired identity document. A name mismatch requires staff review. Staff review is not automatic approval and is not a permanent rejection.

A delegated collector needs a recorded authorization from the reservation holder. The delegated collector must also present their own unexpired identity document. Authorization does not waive that identity-document requirement.

Only the check outcome and reviewer reference are stored in the collection record. The identity document image is not stored. Collection records are available to authorized library staff.

If the identity service times out, the reservation stays active until its original hold deadline. The timeout does not extend the hold. One retry is allowed for this timeout; this instruction does not define retry behavior for other failures.

The service can report an incomplete result. An incomplete result cannot authorize collection. A complete result alone does not establish that every collection requirement has been met.
